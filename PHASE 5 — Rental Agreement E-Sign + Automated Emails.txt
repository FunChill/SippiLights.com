Vercel serverless functions using Resend API, triggered as follows:
1. Booking confirmed (webhook success) → Confirmation email: order summary, event date, delivery expectations, agreement PDF attached, balance due reminder.
2. 3 days before event_date (Vercel cron daily) → Reminder email: delivery details, venue/power checklist, contact number.
3. 1 day after event_date (same cron) → Thank-you email: review request with Google review link placeholder GOOGLE_REVIEW_URL env var, and "book your next event" link.
4. Inquiry received (status 'inquiry' insert) → auto-reply: "Got your request — we respond within 24 hours" + owner notification email to OWNER_EMAIL env var.
Tone for all emails: casually friendly, excited for the customer's event, it's all about their moment. Real copy, no placeholders except env-var links.
